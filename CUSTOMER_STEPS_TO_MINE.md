## 📋 PART 1: GET A CRYPTO WALLET (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Choose Your Coin
Pick one coin to start with:
- **DOGE** 🐕 (Easiest for beginners)
- **BTC** ₿ (Most valuable)
- **ETH** Ξ (Popular & balanced)
- Other options: LTC, ZEC, XMR, RVN, ETC

**👉 Start with DOGE if unsure!**

---

### Step 2: Get a Free Wallet

**For DOGE (Dogecoin):**
1. Go to: **https://www.coinbase.com**
2. Click "Sign Up" (top right)
3. Enter email & create password
4. Verify your email
5. Click "Accounts" (left menu)
6. Click "Create Account"
7. Search for "Dogecoin" or "DOGE"
8. Click "Create Dogecoin Wallet"
9. ✅ Your wallet is ready!

**For BTC (Bitcoin):**
1. Go to: **https://www.coinbase.com**
2. Same steps as above
3. Search for "Bitcoin" or "BTC"
4. Click "Create Bitcoin Wallet"
5. ✅ Done!

**For ETH (Ethereum):**
1. Go to: **https://metamask.io**
2. Click "Download" → "Chrome Extension" (or mobile)
3. Click "Create Wallet"
4. Create password
5. Save your seed phrase somewhere safe
6. ✅ Ready!

**For other coins:**
- Use same wallets above
- Most support multiple coins
- Or Google "[CoinName] wallet free"

---

### Step 3: Find Your Wallet Address

**In Coinbase:**
1. Click on your coin (DOGE, BTC, or ETH)
2. Click "Receive"
3. Copy your address (long string of letters/numbers)
4. Save it somewhere safe (notepad, email, etc.)

**In MetaMask (Ethereum):**
1. Click your name at top
2. Click "Copy Address"
3. Save it

**Example wallet addresses:**
- DOGE: `D7wm3f7g8h9jk0l1m2n3o4p5q6r7s8t9`
- BTC: `1A1z7agoat2zzz6h3k4j5m6n7o8p9q0r`
- ETH: `0x1a2b3c4d5e6f7g8h9i0j1k2l3m4n5o6p`

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** Your address is PUBLIC. It's safe to share. NEVER share your private key/seed phrase!

## 1️⃣ DOGECOIN (DOGE) 🐕
**Best for: Beginners, fastest earnings**

### Setup Steps:
1. Go to https://unmineable.com
2. Click "DOGE" tab
3. Enter your Dogecoin wallet address (from Coinbase, Kraken, etc.)
4. Get your Unmineable ID: `um_xxxxx`
5. Copy & paste into RDCM Mining Bot
6. Start mining!

### What You Need:
- A Dogecoin wallet (free on Coinbase)
- Your public wallet address

### Payout Time:
- Updates: Every 60 seconds
- Withdrawal: Instant to wallet (no minimum)

### Earnings:
- Fastest payout of all coins
- Lower value but more volume
- Perfect for beginners

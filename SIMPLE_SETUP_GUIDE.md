# 🚀 RDCM Mining Bot - Simple Setup (5 Minutes)

## Step 1️⃣ Open App
Go to: **https://mining-bot-deploy.onrender.com**

## Step 2️⃣ Create Account
- Email: your@email.com
- Password: choose any password (6+ characters)

## Step 3️⃣ Get Wallet Address
1. Go to **https://unmineable.com**
2. Pick your coin (DOGE, BTC, ETH, etc.)
3. Enter your crypto wallet address
4. Copy your Unmineable ID (starts with "um_")
5. Paste in RDCM app

## Step 4️⃣ Pick Coin
Click one: DOGE 🐕 | BTC ₿ | ETH Ξ | LTC ₮ | ZEC | XMR | RVN | ETC

## Step 5️⃣ Start Mining
Click **▶️ Start Mining**

## Done! ✅

- Watch earnings appear
- Withdraw anytime via Unmineable
